# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GatewayforAI to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity. Maintainers and
contributors agree to interact in good faith and treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or deliberately acting in bad faith.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any official communication channel — and when representing the
project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a
private security advisory on the repository, or by contacting a maintainer
directly. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially and in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

---

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
